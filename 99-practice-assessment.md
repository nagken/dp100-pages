# Practice Assessment - DP-100

> Microsoft offers a **free** official practice assessment that mirrors the live exam style.

## Take it

[Official Microsoft DP-100 practice assessment ->](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/azure-data-scientist/)

- Free, no-login required for a single attempt
- Multiple-choice with explanations and Microsoft Learn links per item
- Pull questions from the same item bank style as the live exam
- Aim for 80%+ before scheduling the exam

## How to use it well

1. **Take it cold** the first time - establish a baseline.
2. Note every wrong answer's domain. That's your study list.
3. Re-read the linked Microsoft Learn module for each missed concept.
4. After 1-2 days of focused study, retake. Should jump 10-20%.
5. After hitting 80%+ twice in a row, schedule the live exam.

## Day-of-exam reminders

- 2 hours timed, ~50-60 questions, plus case studies and labs.
- Mark any question you're unsure about and come back at the end.
- Read every word in the scenario - esp. compute size, region, identity model.
- "Most cost-effective" usually means **serverless** or **batch endpoint**.
- "Lowest latency" usually means **managed online endpoint**.
- "Without storing credentials" usually means **managed identity / OIDC**.
- "Without writing scoring code" means **mlflow_model**.
